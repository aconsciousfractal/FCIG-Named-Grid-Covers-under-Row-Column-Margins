## 1. Verdict

The bounded-mechanism task has a concrete positive closure, but in a narrower
and more honest form than a shape classifier:

> For a fixed selected set of named grid pieces, row+column support-exactness,
> fiber purity and 2-replacement repair-exactness are fixed-parameter
> tractable in the co-area
> `q = |T| - area(largest selected piece)`.

Strip width is not needed.  The proof is a residual-state kernel: after a
largest-piece placement is frozen, every residual placement participating in
a margin match lies on at most `q` active rows and `q` active columns, hence
inside at most `q^2` cells.

This closes the requested parameterised-decidability lane.  It does **not**
yet give a simple geometric condition explaining why C32 is pure, C46 is
repairable and P49 traps.

## 2. Exact model and scope

Let:

- `T` be a finite target in the square grid;
- `E = {P_0,...,P_k}` be nonempty named pieces with total area `|T|`;
- legal poses be generated by a fixed finite orientation group and
  translations, and required to lie inside `T`;
- `X` be the joint row-and-column X-ray;
- `F_E` be the one-copy placement tuples whose X-rays sum to `X(T)`;
- `T_E` be the tuples in `F_E` whose coverage is exactly `1_T`;
- the repair graph join two nodes precisely when two named placements change.

Freeze one largest named piece `L`, breaking ties deterministically, and put

```text
q = |T| - |L|.
```

The theorem is per selected `E`, or linear in an explicitly supplied list of
selected sets.  It is not an FPT enumeration theorem for an implicitly
specified family of all sublibraries.

## 3. Proof

### Lemma 1 — active lines

Fix a legal placement `A` of `L` and write

```text
b = X(T) - X(A).
```

The row coordinates of `b` are the row counts of `T - A`, and therefore sum
to `q`.  At most `q` of them are positive.  The same is true for the column
coordinates.

If a residual placement `Q` occurs in a tuple whose residual X-rays sum to
`b`, then `X(Q) <= b` coordinatewise.  Hence `Q` uses no inactive row and no
inactive column.  It is contained in the intersection of at most `q` active
rows and `q` active columns: an active grid of at most `q^2` target cells.

### Lemma 2 — bounded margin states

There are at most `q` residual pieces because they are nonempty and have total
area `q`.  A named-piece dynamic programme stores partial X-ray sums bounded
by `b`.

The exact rectangular bound on the number of states is

```text
B(b) = product_j (b_j + 1).
```

The preregistration used the loose bound `(q+1)^(2q)`.  The proof gives the
stronger bound

```text
B(b) <= 4^q:
```

on each of the row and column sides,
`product_i (b_i+1) <= product_i 2^(b_i) = 2^q`.

Thus margin feasibility for fixed `A` is decidable by a state space depending
only on `q`, times the number of inspected placement records.

### Lemma 3 — bounded tiling states

For an exact tiling, every residual placement is contained in the actual
`q`-cell complement `T-A`.  A named-piece subset dynamic programme therefore
uses at most `2^q` coverage masks.  It reaches the full mask exactly when the
residual named pieces tile `T-A`.

### Lemma 4 — bounded full fiber

For fixed `A`, every residual placement is a subset of an active grid of at
most `q^2` cells.  Hence each residual piece has at most `2^(q^2)` distinct
participating placements.  With at most `q` residual pieces, the complete
residual tuple space is at most `2^(q^3)`.

Legal placements of `L` are polynomially enumerable: for each fixed
orientation, anchor one fixed piece cell at every target cell and test
containment.  Therefore the entire fiber has

```text
V <= M_L * 2^(q^3)
```

nodes, with `M_L` polynomial in the cell-list input.  Testing all node pairs
constructs the 2-replacement graph in

```text
O(q * M_L^2 * 2^(2q^3))
```

time.  Labeling tilings and traversing components then decides fiber purity,
repair-exactness and the maximum finite repair radius.

This is an FPT bound: the exponent on the ordinary input size is constant.
It is intentionally not presented as a practical worst-case bound.

## 4. Necessary-and-sufficient computational criterion

For every largest placement `A`, compute:

```text
M_A = the margin DP reaches b;
T_A = the subset DP reaches T-A.
```

Then:

```text
F_E nonempty  iff  OR_A M_A
T_E nonempty  iff  OR_A T_A
```

and support-exactness for the selected row is exactly

```text
(OR_A M_A) iff (OR_A T_A).
```

Reconstructing all accepting margin-DP paths gives `F_E`; coverage tests give
`T_E`; the predeclared 2-replacement graph decides purity and repair.  No
feature was selected from the C32/C46/P49 verdicts.

## 7. Prior-art and novelty boundary

The targeted source lock establishes:

- unrestricted fixed-tile row/column packing tomography is NP-complete for
  every non-bar tile;
- generic Exact Cover parameterised only by the number of chosen sets is
  W[1]-hard/complete;
- geometric set systems do not automatically remove that hardness.

There is no contradiction.  P42's additional near-spanning named piece forces
all margin-participating residual poses into a `q^2`-cell active universe.

No directly matching co-area formulation was found in the targeted search,
but the search is not sufficient for a novelty claim.  The bounded-universe
DP ingredients are standard.  The honest status is:

## 8. What is closed and what remains genuinely open

### Closed now

1. A necessary-and-sufficient bounded algorithm for support-exactness in the
   declared grid model.
2. FPT decidability of fiber purity.
3. FPT decidability of 2-replacement repair-exactness and computation of its
   finite radius.
4. One uniform implementation that reproduces the three exact behaviours
   without tuning.
5. An explicit full-fiber bound
   `V(E) <= h(q+1)(h q^2)^q = 2^(O(q log q))`.
6. Existence of a co-area-only radius bound:
   `rho(E) <= h(q+1)(h q^2)^q-1`.
7. FPT decision and compressed classification for the implicit
   all-sublibrary problem under the exact participation-type contract.

### Still open

1. **Structural explanation.** The algorithm decides the C32/C46/P49
   contrast but does not turn it into a short shape criterion or forbidden
   pattern theorem.
2. **Sharp radius.** The existence question is closed, but polynomial,
   linear and sharp bounds remain open.
3. **Placement-coloured structure.** The quotient
   `ker_Z[U;X]/<two-piece moves>`, its nonnegative component splitting and
   the energy barrier `beta` have not been computed.
4. **Explicit named enumeration.** Output may have size `binomial(N,q)`;
   a duplicate-free Delay-FPT implementation is not yet a P42 artifact.
5. **C34.** The lattice-aligned atom census and continuous completeness are
   now separate gates; neither is closed.
6. **Novelty.** A systematic literature audit is still required before any
   claim-curator proposal.

## 9. Decision

Do not spend the next cycle on the now-closed radius-existence or
implicit-decision questions. The best continuation is the placement-coloured
quotient plus nonnegative component classification, recording tilings,
`Phi`, barrier `beta` and automorphism orbits per component.

C34-L is a separate engineering route and C34-G a separate continuous
geometry obligation. Paper preparation remains premature until the structural
lane or a systematic novelty audit produces a defensible contribution.
